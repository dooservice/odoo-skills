# Coverage

Generated by `python3 tools/build_coverage.py` from Odoo's Community source of each version;
do not edit by hand.

- **Functional**: every addon under `addons/` and `odoo/addons/`, `l10n_*`, `test_*`, `hw_*`
  and `theme_*` left out. Covered: a recipe of `odoo-functional` declares it in
  `tools/coverage/recipes.toml`. Technical: no flow of its own, listed with its reason in
  `tools/coverage/technical.toml`. Missing: neither.
- **Development**: the framework items read from the source. Covered: the version's
  `odoo-guidelines`, `odoo-web-guidelines` or `odoo-security` name the item as code
  (`fields.Char`, `api.depends`, `<kanban`, `.search`, `type='http'`, `auth='public'`,
  `category("fields")`, `TransactionCase`, `@tagged`).
